QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions.

How do bookings work?

Choose a shoot, select a package, send your preferred date and time, then wait for the photographer to confirm availability before payment.

Is my booking confirmed immediately?

No. Your first submission is a request. The photographer confirms the slot before payment instructions appear.

Can I book an on-location shoot?

Yes. Choose On Location when booking and enter the area or venue. Any travel/location fee is confirmed by the photographer.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. One free reschedule is planned, subject to photographer confirmation and availability.

Can I send inspiration photos?

Yes. Clients can upload up to four inspiration photos when submitting a booking request.

How will I receive my finished photos?

Your private download link will appear in My Bookings after the photographer adds it.
